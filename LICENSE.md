# Data licence & attribution

The data in this repository is derived from **Province of Manitoba open
data** (Manitoba GeoPortal / Manitoba Land Initiative), redistributed as
generated GeoJSON/JSON shards for the Manitoba parcel-search web app.

## Source

- **Roll Entry** parcel polygons and attributes — Manitoba GeoPortal
  (geoportal.gov.mb.ca), Province of Manitoba.
- Derived/generated shards (`rollentry-snapshot/`, `assessment/`,
  `parcel-masc/`, `masc/`, river-lot and section-grid layers) are produced
  by the build scripts in the
  [manitoba-opendata-parcelsearch](https://github.com/jayschellenberg/manitoba-opendata-parcelsearch)
  app repo from the above sources.

## Licence

The underlying Province of Manitoba data is made available under the
**Open Government Licence – Manitoba**. Redistribution here is subject to
that licence. See the Manitoba GeoPortal / manitoba.ca open-data terms for
the authoritative licence text.

## Required attribution

> Contains information licensed under the Open Government Licence – Manitoba.

This statement, and the original source dates, are recorded in the app's
exports and provenance metadata.
